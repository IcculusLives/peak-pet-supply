If the button doesn't work in your browser: click inside the box, press ⌘A (Mac) or Ctrl+A (Windows) to select it, then ⌘C / Ctrl+C.

Put it in Gmail (about 2 minutes)
  1. In Gmail on a computer, click the ⚙ gear (top right) → See all settings. You'll be on the General tab.
  2. Scroll down to Signature → click Create new → name it Peak → Create.
  3. Click into the empty signature box and paste (⌘V / Ctrl+V). The logo and layout come across as-is.
  4. Just below, under Signature defaults, choose Peak for both For new emails use and On reply/forward use.
  5. Scroll to the bottom and click Save Changes. Send yourself a quick test email to see it.

Using the Gmail app on your phone? Mobile signatures are plain text only — set this one on a computer and it will show on emails you send from the desktop; the phone app uses its own simpler signature.
